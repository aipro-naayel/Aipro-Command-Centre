My quality bar

Every project should answer these questions before it gets called “good”.

  • What is the actual task?
  • What does success look like?
  • What is the baseline?
  • Where does it fail?
  • Who carries the risk?

A sensible order

A loop, not a ladder. Return to earlier steps whenever a project exposes a gap.
